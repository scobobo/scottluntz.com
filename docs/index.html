The Shape of the Modern Pitch

Research paper, August 2026

A study of 7,483,321 Statcast pitches from 2015 to 2025. Pitch shape, meaning how much the ball moves and the angle it arrives at, explains 4.5 times more of the variation in run value than velocity does, and spin rate adds nothing measurable to run value once velocity is accounted for. The study began from the opposite hypothesis, and the paper says so.

Pitch Shape Explorer

Interactive dashboard, 2015–2026 seasons

Scores 15,358 pitcher-seasons against the whiff rate their pitch shape predicts, and names the pitchers whose results are running ahead of it or behind it. Tested by predicting each season from only the seasons before it: on four-seam fastballs it beat plain regression to the mean in 9 of 9 seasons. Search any pitcher, or open his whole arsenal.

About

I'm a passionate baseball fan and a technologist by trade. This work started with a question I couldn't let go of: had spin rate really replaced velocity as the thing that separates pitchers? I pulled every tracked pitch since 2015 to find out. The answer was no, and the paper says so.

I use AI as a core part of my research workflow, which lets me go from a question to a tested, documented answer quickly. The questions, the standard of evidence and the conclusions are mine, and the paper describes the workflow in full.

Everything here is built from public data and all of the code is open, so every number on this site can be checked.
